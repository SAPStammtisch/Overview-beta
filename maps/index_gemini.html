The Concept

Informal. Community-Driven. Open to All.

A "Stammtisch" is a traditional German concept: a regular's table in a local pub where people gather for informal conversation. We've taken this spirit and applied it to the global SAP ecosystem.

Whether you are a developer, consultant, architect, or just curious about SAP, you are welcome. There are no sales pitches, no rigid agendas—just a group of passionate people meeting in cities across the world.
